Description

The BreatheEasy 2000 Mk6 is designed for spaces up to 275 square feet, making it suitable for bedrooms, offices, or small living areas. It features a True HEPA filter that captures airborne particles, including dust, pollen, and pet dander. Operating at a noise level of 49 dB, it provides a relatively quiet experience. The filter has a lifespan of approximately 6 months, ensuring regular maintenance is manageable. Additionally, the unit includes app control functionality, allowing users to monitor and adjust settings remotely for convenience.
