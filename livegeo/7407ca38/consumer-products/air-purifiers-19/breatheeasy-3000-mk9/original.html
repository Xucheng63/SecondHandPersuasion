Description

The BreatheEasy 3000 Mk9 is designed for medium to large rooms, providing coverage of up to 675 square feet. It operates at a noise level of 23 dB, making it suitable for quiet environments. The unit does not utilize a True HEPA filter, and the filter has a lifespan of approximately 6 months before needing replacement. Additionally, users can control the air purifier remotely through a dedicated app, allowing for convenient adjustments and monitoring.
