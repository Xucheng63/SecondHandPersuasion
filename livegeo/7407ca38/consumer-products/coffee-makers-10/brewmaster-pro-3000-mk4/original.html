Description

The BrewMaster Pro 3000 Mk4 is a drip coffee maker designed for those who enjoy freshly ground coffee. It features a built-in grinder, allowing users to grind beans directly before brewing. The carafe has a capacity of 56 oz, making it suitable for multiple servings. The brew time is approximately 11 minutes, providing a quick and efficient coffee-making experience. However, it does not include a programmable timer, so users must manually operate the device for each brew. This model combines convenience with functionality for everyday coffee preparation.
