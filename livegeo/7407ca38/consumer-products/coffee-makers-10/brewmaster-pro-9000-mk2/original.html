Description

The BrewMaster Pro 9000 Mk2 is a coffee maker designed for espresso enthusiasts. It features a carafe capacity of 32 ounces, making it suitable for multiple servings. The unit is equipped with a programmable timer, allowing users to set their brew time in advance for convenience. While it does not include a built-in grinder, it is designed specifically for brewing espresso. The brewing process takes approximately 9 minutes to complete, ensuring that you can enjoy a fresh cup of espresso without long wait times.
