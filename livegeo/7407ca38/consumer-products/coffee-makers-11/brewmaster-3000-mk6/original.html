Description

The BrewMaster 3000 Mk6 is a French press coffee maker designed for those who appreciate a classic brewing method. It features a carafe capacity of 28 ounces, making it suitable for small gatherings or personal use. The device includes a programmable timer, allowing users to set the brewing time in advance for convenience. The BrewMaster 3000 Mk6 does not have a built-in grinder, so users will need to provide their own freshly ground coffee. The total brew time for a full pot is approximately 4 minutes, ensuring a quick and efficient brewing process.
