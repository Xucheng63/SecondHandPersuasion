Description

The BrewMaster 3000 Mk8 is a French press coffee maker designed for those who appreciate a classic brewing method. It features a carafe capacity of 24 ounces, making it suitable for small gatherings or personal use. The device includes a programmable timer, allowing users to set the brew time in advance for convenience. The brewing process takes approximately 6 minutes, ensuring a consistent extraction of flavors. Please note that this model does not include a built-in grinder, requiring users to have pre-ground coffee or a separate grinder for their brewing needs.
