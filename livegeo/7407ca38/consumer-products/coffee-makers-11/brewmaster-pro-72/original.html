Description

The BrewMaster Pro 72 is a single-serve pod coffee maker designed for convenience and efficiency. It features a carafe capacity of 72 ounces, making it suitable for multiple servings. This model does not include a programmable timer, allowing for straightforward operation without complex settings. Additionally, it does not have a built-in grinder, which means users will need to use pre-ground coffee or coffee pods. The brew time is approximately 9 minutes, providing a quick brewing process for those who need their coffee in a timely manner.
