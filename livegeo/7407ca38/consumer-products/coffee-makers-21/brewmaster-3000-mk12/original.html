Description

The BrewMaster 3000 Mk12 is a French press coffee maker designed for those who appreciate a traditional brewing method. It features a carafe capacity of 16 ounces, making it suitable for small gatherings or personal use. The device does not include a built-in grinder, so users will need to provide their own ground coffee. The BrewMaster 3000 Mk12 does not have a programmable timer, allowing for straightforward operation without complex settings. Brew time is approximately 9 minutes, ensuring a consistent extraction of flavors while providing a simple, hands-on brewing experience.
