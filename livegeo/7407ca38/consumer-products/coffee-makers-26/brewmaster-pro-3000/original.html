Description

The BrewMaster Pro 3000 is a compact espresso coffee maker designed for ease of use. It has a carafe capacity of 32 ounces, making it suitable for small gatherings or individual use. This model does not feature a programmable timer, which means it operates on a manual basis. Additionally, it does not include a built-in grinder, allowing users to select their preferred coffee grounds. The BrewMaster Pro 3000 brews espresso in approximately 10 minutes, providing a straightforward brewing experience for espresso enthusiasts.
