Description

The Auralis Wave 400 headphones feature a wireless design with an on-ear fit, weighing 400 grams. They offer a battery life of up to 26 hours for extended listening sessions. These headphones do not include noise cancelling technology, making them suitable for users who prefer ambient sound. The device is compatible with various Bluetooth-enabled devices, providing a reliable audio experience without the hassle of cables.
