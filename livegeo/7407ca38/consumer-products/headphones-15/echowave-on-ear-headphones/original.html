Description

The EchoWave On-Ear Headphones offer a battery life of 12 hours, providing extended listening without the need for frequent recharging. These headphones feature active noise cancelling technology, allowing users to minimize outside noise for a more immersive audio experience. Weighing 270 grams, they are designed for comfort during prolonged use. The on-ear fit provides a snug sensation, ensuring stability while listening. Please note that these headphones are not wireless, requiring a wired connection for audio playback.
