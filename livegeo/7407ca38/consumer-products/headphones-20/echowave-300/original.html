Description

The EchoWave 300 headphones feature a robust over-ear design, ensuring a comfortable fit for extended listening sessions. Weighing 280 grams, they are designed for both durability and ease of use. With a battery life of 46 hours, these headphones are suitable for long periods of use without the need for frequent recharging. Please note that these headphones do not include noise cancelling technology and are not wireless, making them ideal for users who prefer a traditional wired connection.
