Description

The Auralis EchoBeats 1000 are in-ear wireless headphones designed for everyday use. Weighing 190 grams, they provide a lightweight experience for users. The headphones offer a battery life of up to 37 hours on a single charge, ensuring extended listening sessions without the need for frequent recharging. These headphones do not feature noise cancelling technology, making them suitable for environments where external sounds are desired. The EchoBeats 1000 are compatible with a variety of devices, providing convenience for users who prefer a wireless experience.
