Description

The TechGuard ProPack 14 Mk3 is a functional laptop backpack designed for everyday use. It has a capacity of 33 liters and can accommodate laptops with a maximum size of 14 inches. The backpack is made from waterproof materials, providing protection against the elements. Weighing 2.8 pounds, it is lightweight and easy to carry. Please note that this model does not include a USB charging port.
