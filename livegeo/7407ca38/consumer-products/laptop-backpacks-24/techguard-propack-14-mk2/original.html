Description

The TechGuard ProPack 14 Mk2 is designed for those seeking a reliable and functional laptop backpack. With a capacity of 29 liters, it accommodates laptops with a maximum size of 14 inches. The backpack features a waterproof construction, ensuring your belongings remain protected in wet conditions. Weighing 2.8 pounds, it offers a lightweight option for daily use. The TechGuard ProPack 14 Mk2 does not include a USB charging port, making it a straightforward choice for users who prioritize simplicity and practicality in their gear.
