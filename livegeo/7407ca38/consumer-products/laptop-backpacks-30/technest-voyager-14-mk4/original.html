Description

The TechNest Voyager 14 Mk4 is a compact laptop backpack designed for everyday use. It has a capacity of 23 liters and can accommodate laptops with a maximum size of 14 inches. The backpack features a USB charging port for convenient device charging on the go. Weighing 1.9 pounds, it is lightweight and easy to carry. Please note, this model is not waterproof, so it is recommended to keep it dry in wet conditions. Its practical design makes it suitable for students and professionals alike.
