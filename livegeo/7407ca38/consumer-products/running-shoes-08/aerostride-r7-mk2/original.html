Description

The AeroStride R7 Mk2 running shoes are designed for road use and feature a minimal cushioning system. Each shoe weighs 6.9 ounces, making them lightweight for runners. The shoes are waterproof, offering protection against wet conditions. With an 11 mm heel-to-toe drop, the R7 aims to provide a balanced running experience. These shoes are suitable for various training sessions and everyday wear, catering to runners who prefer a more direct connection to the ground while still enjoying some protection from the elements.
