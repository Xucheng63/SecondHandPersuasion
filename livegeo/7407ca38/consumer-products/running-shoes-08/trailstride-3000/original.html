Description

The TrailStride 3000 running shoes are designed for trail enthusiasts seeking a lightweight option. Each shoe weighs 9.9 oz, providing a minimal feel for those who prefer a closer connection to the ground. The cushioning is minimal, allowing for a more natural foot movement on rugged terrain. These shoes are not waterproof, making them suitable for dry conditions rather than wet environments. With a heel-to-toe drop of 3 mm, they offer a low-profile design that can help promote a more efficient running style on various trail surfaces.
