Description

The TrailStride 500 is designed for trail running with a minimal cushioning approach. Each shoe weighs 12.0 oz, providing a lightweight feel during runs. The heel-to-toe drop is 5 mm, contributing to a natural stride. This model is not waterproof, making it suitable for dry conditions and well-maintained trails. The construction emphasizes durability and grip, catering to runners who prefer a more connected experience with the terrain.
