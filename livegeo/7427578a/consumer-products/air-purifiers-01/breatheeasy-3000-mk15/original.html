Description

The BreatheEasy 3000 Mk15 is designed for medium to large rooms, providing effective air purification for spaces up to 675 square feet. Operating at a noise level of 31 dB, it ensures quiet performance suitable for bedrooms or living areas. The filter has a lifespan of approximately 6 months, depending on usage and environmental factors. This model does not include app control capabilities, offering a straightforward operation without the need for smartphone integration.
