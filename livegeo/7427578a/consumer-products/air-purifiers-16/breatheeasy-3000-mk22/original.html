Description

The BreatheEasy 3000 Mk22 is designed for medium to large rooms, providing effective air purification for spaces up to 725 square feet. It features a True HEPA filter, which captures 99.97% of airborne particles as small as 0.3 microns. Operating at a noise level of 48 dB, it is suitable for use in bedrooms or living areas without being disruptive. The filter has a lifespan of approximately 11 months, ensuring consistent performance over time. Please note that this model does not include app control functionality, making it straightforward to operate without the need for smartphone integration.
