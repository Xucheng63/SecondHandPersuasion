Description

The BreatheEasy 3000 Mk11 is designed for spaces up to 300 square feet. It operates at a noise level of 29 dB, making it suitable for use in quiet environments. The unit does not utilize a True HEPA filter but features an alternative filtration system. The filter has a lifespan of 12 months, requiring annual replacement. Additionally, the BreatheEasy 3000 Mk11 offers app control functionality, allowing users to manage settings and monitor air quality remotely.
