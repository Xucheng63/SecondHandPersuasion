Description

The BreatheEasy 3000 Mk2 is designed for spaces up to 900 square feet. It operates at a noise level of 40 dB, making it suitable for use in various environments without being disruptive. This air purifier does not utilize a True HEPA filter, but it features a filter with a lifespan of 11 months, ensuring long-term usability before replacement is necessary. Additionally, users can control the device through a dedicated app, allowing for convenient management of settings and performance from a smartphone.
