Description

The BreatheEasy 550 is designed for rooms up to 550 square feet, featuring a True HEPA filter that captures airborne particles effectively. The filter has a lifespan of approximately 10 months, ensuring consistent performance before needing replacement. This model does not include app control, offering a straightforward approach to air purification without additional smart features.
