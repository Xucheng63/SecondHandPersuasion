Description

The BrewMaster Pro 12 coffee maker features a 12 oz carafe capacity, making it suitable for small servings of coffee. It includes a programmable timer, allowing users to set a brew time in advance. The built-in grinder enables users to grind their coffee beans fresh for each brew, enhancing the flavor of the coffee. This model utilizes a drip brew method, which is standard for many coffee makers. The total brew time is approximately 11 minutes, providing a convenient option for users who want freshly brewed coffee without a lengthy wait.
