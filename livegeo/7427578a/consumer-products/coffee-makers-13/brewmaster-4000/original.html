Description

The BrewMaster 4000 is a drip coffee maker designed for simplicity and efficiency. It features a carafe capacity of 40 ounces, making it suitable for multiple servings. The average brew time for a full carafe is approximately 9 minutes, allowing for a quick and easy coffee preparation experience.
