Description

The BrewMaster Pro 64 coffee maker features a 64 oz carafe capacity, making it suitable for brewing larger quantities of coffee. It includes a programmable timer, allowing users to set a specific brew time in advance. The built-in grinder ensures fresh coffee grounds are used for each brew, enhancing the overall flavor. This model specializes in espresso preparation, delivering a rich and concentrated coffee experience. The brew time is approximately 10 minutes, providing efficiency without compromising quality.
