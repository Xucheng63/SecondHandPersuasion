Description

The BrewMaster Pro 2000 Mk2 is designed for single-serve pod coffee brewing. It features a carafe capacity of 32 ounces, making it suitable for individual servings or small gatherings. This model does not include a programmable timer, allowing for straightforward operation without pre-set brewing options. Additionally, it does not have a built-in grinder, so users will need to use pre-ground coffee pods. The brewing process takes approximately 13 minutes from start to finish, ensuring a consistent cup of coffee with each use.
