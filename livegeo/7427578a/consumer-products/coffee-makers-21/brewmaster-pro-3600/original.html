Description

The BrewMaster Pro 3600 is a coffee maker designed for espresso enthusiasts. It features a carafe capacity of 36 ounces, making it suitable for multiple servings. The unit is equipped with a programmable timer, allowing users to set their brew time in advance. A built-in grinder is included, providing fresh coffee grounds for each brew. The BrewMaster Pro 3600 offers a brew time of approximately 5 minutes, ensuring a quick and efficient coffee preparation experience.
