Description

The BrewMaster Pro 3000 Mk6 is a coffee maker designed for espresso enthusiasts. It features a carafe capacity of 52 ounces, allowing for multiple servings in one brew cycle. The programmable timer enables users to set their brew time in advance for convenience. However, it does not include a built-in grinder, so pre-ground coffee must be used. The machine brews espresso in approximately 14 minutes, making it suitable for those who appreciate a strong coffee experience without extensive wait times.
