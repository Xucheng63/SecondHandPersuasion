Description

The Auralis Echo 400 headphones feature a robust battery life of 39 hours, providing extended usage without frequent recharging. Designed with an on-ear fit, they offer comfort for prolonged listening sessions. The headphones are equipped with noise cancelling technology, helping to reduce ambient sounds for a more immersive audio experience. Weighing 400 grams, they are relatively lightweight, making them suitable for daily use. It is important to note that the Auralis Echo 400 headphones are not wireless, requiring a wired connection for operation.
