Description

The TechGuard ProPack 13 is a compact laptop backpack designed for everyday use. It features a capacity of 20 liters, accommodating laptops with a maximum size of 13 inches. The backpack is made from waterproof materials, ensuring that your belongings remain dry in wet conditions. Additionally, it includes a USB charging port, allowing for convenient charging of devices on the go. Weighing in at 1.2 pounds, it is lightweight and easy to carry, making it suitable for students and professionals alike.
