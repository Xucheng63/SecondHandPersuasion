Description

The TechHaven UrbanPack 16 Mk2 is designed for everyday use with a capacity of 17 liters. It accommodates laptops with a maximum size of 16 inches. The backpack features a built-in USB charging port for convenient device charging on the go. Weighing 3.4 pounds, it is lightweight and easy to carry. Please note that this backpack is not waterproof, making it suitable for dry conditions. The UrbanPack 16 combines practicality and functionality for users seeking a reliable solution for transporting their technology and essentials.
