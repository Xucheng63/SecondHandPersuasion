Description

The AeroStride 2000 Mk2 running shoes are designed for road running with a minimal cushioning setup. Weighing 6.2 ounces per shoe, they provide a lightweight option for runners seeking efficiency. The shoes feature an 8 mm heel-to-toe drop, promoting a natural running gait. Although they are not waterproof, they are engineered for durability and comfort on paved surfaces. Ideal for those who prefer a more grounded feel while running, the AeroStride 2000 Mk2 focuses on simplicity and performance without added bulk.
