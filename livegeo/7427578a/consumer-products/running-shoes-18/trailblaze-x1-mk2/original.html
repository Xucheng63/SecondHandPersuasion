Description

The TrailBlaze X1 Mk2 running shoes are designed for trail enthusiasts seeking maximum cushioning. Each shoe weighs 10.3 oz, providing a balanced feel during runs. These shoes are not waterproof, making them suitable for dry conditions. The 6 mm heel-to-toe drop promotes a natural stride, enhancing comfort on uneven terrain. Ideal for those who regularly engage in trail running, the TrailBlaze X1 Mk2 combines performance with a durable design to withstand various outdoor elements.
