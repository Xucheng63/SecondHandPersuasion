Description

The BreatheEasy 3000 Mk17 is designed for medium to large rooms, providing coverage of up to 700 square feet. It operates at a noise level of 58 dB, making it suitable for various environments. The unit does not utilize a True HEPA filter but includes other filtration technologies to help improve indoor air quality. The filter life is approximately 17 months, ensuring extended usage before replacement is needed. Additionally, the BreatheEasy 3000 Mk17 features app control capabilities, allowing users to monitor and adjust settings remotely for convenience.
