Description

The BreatheEasy 200 is designed for rooms up to 200 square feet, making it suitable for small to medium spaces. It operates at a noise level of 29 dB, allowing for quiet use during day or night. The device features a filter that has a lifespan of 15 months, ensuring efficient air purification over time. It does not include a True HEPA filter and lacks app control functionality, focusing instead on straightforward operation. This air purifier is a practical choice for those seeking simple air quality improvement without advanced features.
