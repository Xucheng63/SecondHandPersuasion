Description

The BreatheEasy 3000 Mk12 is designed for spaces up to 725 square feet, making it suitable for large rooms or open areas. It features a True HEPA filter, which captures 99.97% of airborne particles as small as 0.3 microns. Operating at a noise level of 57 dB, it can function quietly in the background. The filter has a life span of approximately 11 months, ensuring effective air purification over time. Additionally, the device supports app control, allowing users to monitor and adjust settings remotely for convenience.
