Description

The BreatheEasy 3000 Mk3 is designed for medium to large rooms, providing coverage of up to 875 square feet. It operates at a noise level of 59 dB, making it suitable for various environments. The unit does not utilize a True HEPA filter, and the filter has a lifespan of approximately 17 months, ensuring long-term use before replacement is necessary. Additionally, the BreatheEasy 3000 Mk3 does not offer app control features, allowing for straightforward operation without the need for smartphone connectivity.
