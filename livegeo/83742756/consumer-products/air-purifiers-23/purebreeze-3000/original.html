Description

The PureBreeze 3000 is designed for large spaces with a room coverage of up to 1175 square feet. It operates at a noise level of 32 dB, making it suitable for quiet environments. The air purifier features a filter life of six months, allowing for regular maintenance without frequent replacements. It does not utilize a True HEPA filter and lacks app control functionality, providing a straightforward approach to air purification. Ideal for users seeking a basic air cleaning solution, the PureBreeze 3000 offers efficiency without advanced connectivity features.
