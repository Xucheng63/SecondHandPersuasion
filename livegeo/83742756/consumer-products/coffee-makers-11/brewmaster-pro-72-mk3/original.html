Description

The BrewMaster Pro 72 Mk3 coffee maker features a carafe capacity of 72 ounces, allowing you to brew multiple servings at once. It includes a programmable timer, enabling you to set your brew time in advance. The coffee maker utilizes a drip brew method and has a brew time of approximately 13 minutes. Please note that this model does not come with a built-in grinder, so pre-ground coffee is required for operation. The BrewMaster Pro 72 Mk3 is designed for those who appreciate a straightforward brewing process.
