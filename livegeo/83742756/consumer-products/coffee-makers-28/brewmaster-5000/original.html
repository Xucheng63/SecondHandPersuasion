Description

The BrewMaster 5000 is a drip coffee maker designed for efficiency and simplicity. It features a carafe capacity of 60 ounces, allowing for multiple servings in one brew cycle. The machine does not include a built-in grinder, so users will need to provide pre-ground coffee. Brew time is approximately 5 minutes, ensuring a quick preparation for your coffee needs. The BrewMaster 5000 does not come with a programmable timer, making it suitable for straightforward brewing without advanced scheduling features.
