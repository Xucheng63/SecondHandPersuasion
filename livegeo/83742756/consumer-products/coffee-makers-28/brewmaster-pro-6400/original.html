Description

The BrewMaster Pro 6400 is a coffee maker designed for espresso enthusiasts. It features a carafe capacity of 64 ounces, allowing for multiple servings in one brew cycle. The programmable timer enables users to set their brew time in advance for convenience. However, it does not include a built-in grinder, so users will need to prepare their coffee grounds separately. The machine brews espresso with a brew time of approximately 12 minutes, making it suitable for those who prefer a strong coffee experience.
