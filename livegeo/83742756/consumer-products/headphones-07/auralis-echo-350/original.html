Description

The Auralis Echo 350 headphones feature a wireless design with an over-ear fit, providing a comfortable listening experience. Weighing 350 grams, they are designed for prolonged use. The headphones offer a battery life of up to 42 hours, allowing for extended listening sessions without the need for frequent recharging. Please note that these headphones do not include noise cancelling functionality, making them suitable for users who prefer to remain aware of their surroundings while listening.
