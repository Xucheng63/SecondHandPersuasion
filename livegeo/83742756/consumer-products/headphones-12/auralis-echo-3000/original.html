Description

The Auralis Echo 3000 headphones feature a wireless design and an over-ear fit, providing comfort during extended use. Weighing 330 grams, they are suitable for various listening environments. The headphones offer a battery life of up to 48 hours, allowing for prolonged playback without the need for frequent recharging. It is important to note that these headphones do not include noise cancelling technology, making them suitable for users who prefer ambient sound awareness while listening.
