Description

The EchoWave In-Ear Wireless Headphones Mk2 feature a lightweight design at 200 grams and provide a battery life of up to 25 hours on a single charge. These headphones are designed for an in-ear fit, ensuring comfort during extended use. They do not include noise cancelling technology, allowing ambient sounds to be heard while listening to audio. The headphones connect wirelessly, providing convenience and freedom of movement for users. Ideal for casual listening, these headphones balance performance and practicality without the added complexity of advanced noise reduction features.
