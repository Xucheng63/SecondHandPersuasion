Description

The Auralis EchoBeats 3000 are in-ear wireless headphones designed for everyday use. Weighing 410 grams, they provide a lightweight option for users seeking comfort during extended wear. The headphones feature a battery life of up to 38 hours, allowing for prolonged listening sessions without the need for frequent recharging. It is important to note that these headphones do not include noise cancelling technology, making them suitable for users who prefer to remain aware of their surroundings. The EchoBeats 3000 combine practicality and functionality, catering to those who value convenience in their audio experience.
