Description

The Auralis Echo 260 headphones feature a lightweight design at 260 grams and provide an on-ear fit for comfortable listening. These wireless headphones offer a battery life of up to 26 hours, allowing for extended use without frequent recharging. It is important to note that the Echo 260 does not include noise cancelling technology, making them suitable for users who prefer to be aware of their surroundings while enjoying audio. The combination of their durable build and wireless connectivity makes them a practical choice for everyday listening.
