Description

The TechNest Voyager 13 Mk3 is a functional laptop backpack designed for everyday use. It has a capacity of 38 liters and can accommodate laptops with a maximum size of 13 inches. The backpack features a USB charging port for convenient device charging on the go. Weighing 3.6 pounds, it is manageable for daily commutes or travel. Please note that this backpack is not waterproof, so care should be taken in wet conditions. The design includes multiple compartments for organization and easy access to essentials.
