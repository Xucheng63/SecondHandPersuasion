Description

The TechGuard ProPack 14 Mk4 is designed for those seeking a practical solution for carrying their essentials. With a capacity of 18 liters, it accommodates laptops up to 14 inches in size. The backpack features a waterproof exterior, ensuring your belongings remain protected in wet conditions. Weighing 2.3 pounds, it offers a lightweight option for daily use. The design includes multiple compartments for organization, though it does not include a USB charging port. This backpack is suitable for students and professionals alike, providing functionality without compromising on style.
