Description

The TechNest Voyager 34L is designed for those who need a practical solution for carrying their essentials. It features a capacity of 34 liters and can accommodate a laptop with a maximum size of 13 inches. Weighing 2.5 pounds, it is lightweight for daily use. The backpack is not waterproof, making it less suitable for wet conditions. Additionally, it does not include a USB charging port, focusing instead on straightforward storage options. Ideal for students and professionals alike, the TechNest Voyager provides ample space while maintaining a functional design.
