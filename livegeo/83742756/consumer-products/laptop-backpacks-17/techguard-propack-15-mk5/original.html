Description

The TechGuard ProPack 15 Mk5 is designed for those seeking a reliable and functional laptop backpack. With a capacity of 38 liters, it accommodates laptops up to 15 inches in size. The backpack features a waterproof exterior, ensuring that your belongings remain protected in various weather conditions. Weighing 2.3 pounds, it offers a balance of durability and portability. This model does not include a USB charging port, focusing instead on essential storage and protection for your devices.
