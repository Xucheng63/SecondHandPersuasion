Description

The TechGuard ProPack 17 is a functional laptop backpack designed for everyday use. It has a capacity of 21 liters and can accommodate laptops with a maximum size of 17 inches. The backpack is made from waterproof materials, providing protection against the elements. Weighing 3.4 pounds, it is designed for ease of carrying while maintaining durability. Note that this model does not include a USB charging port. The TechGuard ProPack 17 is suitable for students and professionals seeking a reliable and practical solution for transporting their electronics.
