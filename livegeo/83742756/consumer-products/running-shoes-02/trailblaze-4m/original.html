Description

The TrailBlaze 4M running shoes are designed for trail enthusiasts seeking a lightweight option. Weighing 13.0 ounces per shoe, these shoes offer minimal cushioning for a more connected feel with the ground. They feature a 4 mm heel-to-toe drop, promoting a natural running gait. While not waterproof, the breathable materials help to keep feet comfortable during various outdoor conditions. Ideal for uneven terrains, the TrailBlaze 4M is built to handle the demands of trail running.
