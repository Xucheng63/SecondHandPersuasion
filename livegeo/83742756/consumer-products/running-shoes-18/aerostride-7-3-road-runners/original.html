Description

The AeroStride 7.3 Road Runners are designed for those who prefer a lightweight shoe for road running. Each shoe weighs 7.3 oz, providing a minimal feel during runs. The cushioning is minimal, allowing for a closer connection to the ground, and the heel-to-toe drop measures 3 mm, promoting a natural running gait. This model is not waterproof, making it suitable for dry conditions. Ideal for runners seeking a straightforward, no-frills approach to their footwear.
