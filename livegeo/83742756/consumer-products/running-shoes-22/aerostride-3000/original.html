Description

The AeroStride 3000 running shoes are designed specifically for track use, featuring maximum cushioning to enhance comfort during runs. Each shoe weighs 11.5 ounces, balancing support and performance. With an 11 mm heel-to-toe drop, they provide a stable platform for runners. These shoes are not waterproof, making them suitable for dry conditions on the track. The AeroStride 3000 is ideal for athletes seeking a durable option for training and competition.
