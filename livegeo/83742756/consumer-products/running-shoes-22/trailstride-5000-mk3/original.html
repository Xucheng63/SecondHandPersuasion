Description

The TrailStride 5000 Mk3 running shoes are designed for trail running enthusiasts seeking a balance of comfort and durability. Each shoe weighs 8.6 ounces, providing a lightweight feel for extended runs. The moderate cushioning offers a responsive experience on uneven surfaces, while the 9 mm heel-to-toe drop promotes a natural running gait. These shoes are not waterproof, making them suitable for dry conditions. Ideal for varied terrain, the TrailStride 5000 Mk3 combines functionality with a focus on performance for trail runners.
