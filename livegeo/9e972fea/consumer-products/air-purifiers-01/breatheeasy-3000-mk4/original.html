Description

The BreatheEasy 3000 Mk4 is designed for medium to large rooms, providing effective air purification for spaces up to 475 square feet. It features a True HEPA filter, which captures 99.97% of airborne particles as small as 0.3 microns. The device operates at a noise level of 51 dB, making it suitable for use in living rooms or bedrooms. The filter has a lifespan of approximately 7 months, ensuring extended use before replacement is necessary. Please note that the BreatheEasy 3000 Mk4 does not support app control for remote operation.
