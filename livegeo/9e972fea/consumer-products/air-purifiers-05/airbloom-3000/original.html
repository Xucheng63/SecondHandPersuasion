Description

The AirBloom 3000 is designed for efficient air purification in spaces up to 1000 square feet. It operates at a noise level of 27 dB, making it suitable for use in quiet environments. Users can control the device remotely through a dedicated app, allowing for convenient management of air quality from a smartphone or tablet.
