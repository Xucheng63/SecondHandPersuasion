Description

The BreatheEasy 2000 Mk12 is designed for medium to large rooms, providing coverage of up to 825 square feet. It operates at a noise level of 45 dB, making it suitable for use in various environments. The unit features a filter that requires replacement every four months to maintain its efficiency. Notably, the BreatheEasy 2000 Mk12 does not utilize a True HEPA filter and does not offer app control functionalities. This air purifier is a practical choice for those seeking to improve indoor air quality without the added features of smart technology.
