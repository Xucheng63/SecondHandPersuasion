Description

The PureBreeze 5000 is designed for large spaces with a room coverage of up to 1125 square feet. It operates at a noise level of 32 dB, making it suitable for quiet environments. The air purifier features a filter life of 17 months, ensuring extended use before replacement is necessary. However, it does not include a True HEPA filter and lacks app control functionality, providing a straightforward approach to air purification without additional smart features.
