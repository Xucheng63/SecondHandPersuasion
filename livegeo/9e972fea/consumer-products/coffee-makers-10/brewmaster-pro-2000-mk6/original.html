Description

The BrewMaster Pro 2000 Mk6 is designed for single-serve pod coffee brewing. It features a carafe capacity of 28 ounces, making it suitable for individual servings. The unit does not include a programmable timer, allowing for straightforward operation without advanced scheduling features. Additionally, there is no built-in grinder, meaning users will need to utilize pre-ground coffee pods for brewing. The average brew time for a single serving is approximately 9 minutes, providing a consistent brewing experience.
