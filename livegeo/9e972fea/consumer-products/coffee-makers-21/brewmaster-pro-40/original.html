Description

The BrewMaster Pro 40 is a compact single-serve coffee maker designed for convenience. It features a carafe capacity of 40 ounces, allowing for multiple servings without frequent refills. This model does not include a programmable timer, which means it operates on a manual basis. Additionally, it does not come with a built-in grinder, making it suitable for use with pre-ground coffee or single-serve pods. The brew time for each cup is approximately 7 minutes, providing a quick and efficient brewing experience tailored to individual preferences.
