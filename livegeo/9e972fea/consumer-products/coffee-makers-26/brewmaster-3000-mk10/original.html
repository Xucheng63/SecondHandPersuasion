Description

The BrewMaster 3000 Mk10 is a coffee maker designed for those who enjoy the rich flavor of French press coffee. It features a carafe capacity of 76 oz, making it suitable for larger servings. This model includes a built-in grinder, allowing users to grind fresh coffee beans for each brew. The brew time is approximately 9 minutes, providing a balanced extraction for optimal taste. Please note that this coffee maker does not include a programmable timer, which may require manual operation for brewing times.
