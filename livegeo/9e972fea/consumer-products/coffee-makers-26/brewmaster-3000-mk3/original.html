Description

The BrewMaster 3000 Mk3 is a French press coffee maker designed for those who appreciate a traditional brewing method. It features a carafe capacity of 48 ounces, making it suitable for multiple servings. This model does not include a programmable timer or a built-in grinder, allowing for a straightforward brewing experience. The coffee maker requires approximately 13 minutes to brew, ensuring a rich and full-bodied flavor in each cup. The simple design and functionality make it easy to use for both novice and experienced coffee enthusiasts.
