Description

The BrewMaster 68X is a drip coffee maker designed for simplicity and efficiency. With a carafe capacity of 68 ounces, it is suitable for brewing multiple cups of coffee at once. The programmable timer allows users to set their brew time in advance, enhancing convenience for busy mornings. The coffee maker does not include a built-in grinder, requiring pre-ground coffee for use. Brew time is approximately 3 minutes, making it a practical choice for those seeking a quick and straightforward brewing process.
