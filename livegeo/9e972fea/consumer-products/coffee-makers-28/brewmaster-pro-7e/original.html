Description

The BrewMaster Pro 7E is a coffee maker designed for espresso enthusiasts. It features a carafe capacity of 56 ounces, allowing for multiple servings in one brew cycle. The programmable timer enables users to set their brew time in advance for convenience. However, it does not include a built-in grinder, so pre-ground coffee is required for use. The BrewMaster Pro 7E has a brew time of approximately 7 minutes, making it suitable for those who prefer a quicker preparation process while enjoying a rich espresso experience.
