Description

The EchoWave 200 headphones feature a robust over-ear design, ensuring a comfortable fit for extended listening sessions. Weighing 200 grams, they are designed for everyday use. These headphones offer a battery life of up to 35 hours, allowing for prolonged usage without the need for frequent recharging. The EchoWave 200 does not include noise cancelling capabilities, making them suitable for users who prefer to remain aware of their surroundings. Additionally, these headphones are wired, providing a reliable audio connection without the need for wireless connectivity.
