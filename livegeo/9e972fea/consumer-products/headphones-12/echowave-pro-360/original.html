Description

The EchoWave Pro 360 headphones are designed for comfortable, over-ear use, weighing 360 grams. These wired headphones offer an 8-hour battery life, ensuring extended listening sessions without the need for frequent recharging. Please note that they do not feature noise-cancelling technology, allowing for natural ambient sound to be heard. The EchoWave Pro 360 is a practical choice for users who prefer a wired connection for consistent audio performance.
