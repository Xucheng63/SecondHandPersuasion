Description

The EchoSound On-Ear Headphones offer a straightforward listening experience without the complexities of wireless connectivity. Weighing 350 grams, they are designed for a comfortable on-ear fit. With a battery life of up to 45 hours, these headphones provide extended usage for daily activities. However, they do not feature noise cancelling technology, allowing ambient sounds to be heard while listening to audio. Ideal for those who prefer a wired connection, the EchoSound headphones cater to users who prioritize long battery life and a reliable physical connection.
