Description

The Auralis EchoBeats 40 are wireless in-ear headphones designed for extended use. They feature a battery life of up to 40 hours on a single charge, allowing for prolonged listening sessions. With a weight of 410 grams, they are lightweight and portable, making them suitable for everyday wear. These headphones do not include noise cancelling functionality, providing an open sound experience. The design offers a comfortable fit for various ear sizes, ensuring a stable listening experience during activities.
