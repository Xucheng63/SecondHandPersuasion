Description

The TechHaven UrbanPack 36L is designed for everyday use, offering a capacity of 36 liters. It accommodates laptops with a maximum size of 15 inches. The backpack weighs 3.9 pounds, making it manageable for daily commuting or travel. While it features a sleek design, it is important to note that the backpack is not waterproof and does not include a USB charging port. The UrbanPack is suited for individuals seeking a functional and spacious backpack for their tech gear and personal items.
