Description

The TechNest Voyager 17 is a compact laptop backpack designed for everyday use. It has a capacity of 20 liters, providing ample space for carrying essentials. The backpack accommodates laptops with a maximum size of 17 inches, making it suitable for most standard devices. Weighing 1.3 pounds, it is lightweight and easy to carry. Please note that this backpack is not waterproof and does not include a USB charging port. The TechNest Voyager 17 is a practical choice for students and professionals seeking a simple, functional backpack.
