Description

The TechNest Voyager 16L Backpack is designed for everyday use, featuring a capacity of 16 liters and accommodating laptops up to 16 inches in size. Weighing 2.8 pounds, it provides a lightweight option for carrying your essentials. The backpack is not waterproof, making it suitable for dry conditions. It does not come with a USB charging port, focusing instead on a streamlined design without additional electronic features. The TechNest Voyager is a practical choice for students and professionals seeking a functional and simple backpack.
