Description

The TechNest Voyager 41L Backpack is designed for those who need a practical solution for carrying their essentials. It features a capacity of 41 liters and can accommodate laptops up to 16 inches in size. Weighing 2.2 pounds, it offers a lightweight option for daily use. The backpack is not waterproof, making it suitable for dry conditions. Additionally, it does not include a USB charging port, focusing instead on straightforward functionality and storage.
