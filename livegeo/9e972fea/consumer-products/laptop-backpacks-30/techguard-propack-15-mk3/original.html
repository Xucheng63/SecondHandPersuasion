Description

The TechGuard ProPack 15 Mk3 is designed for everyday use with a capacity of 18 liters. It accommodates laptops with a maximum size of 15 inches. The backpack features a waterproof exterior, providing protection against moisture and light rain. Weighing 1.7 pounds, it is lightweight and easy to carry. However, it does not include a USB charging port for electronic devices. This backpack combines functionality and durability, making it suitable for students, professionals, and travelers.
