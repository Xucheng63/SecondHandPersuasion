Description

The TechNest Voyager 19L Backpack is designed for everyday use, featuring a capacity of 19 liters and accommodating laptops up to 16 inches in size. Weighing 3.8 pounds, it provides a balance of portability and storage. The backpack is not waterproof, making it suitable for dry conditions only. It does not come with a USB charging port, focusing instead on a straightforward design that meets basic storage needs.
