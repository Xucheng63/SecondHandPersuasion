Description

The AeroStride R2 running shoes are designed for road use, weighing 8.7 ounces per shoe. These shoes feature a moderate level of cushioning to provide a balanced running experience. With a 2 mm heel-to-toe drop, they offer a lower profile that promotes a natural foot movement. It is important to note that these shoes are not waterproof, making them suitable for dry conditions. The combination of lightweight construction and moderate cushioning makes the AeroStride R2 a practical choice for everyday runners.
