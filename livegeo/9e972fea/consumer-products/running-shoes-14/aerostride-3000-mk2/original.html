Description

The AeroStride 3000 Mk2 running shoes are designed specifically for road running. Each shoe weighs 8.2 ounces, providing a lightweight feel during runs. The shoes feature maximum cushioning to enhance comfort over longer distances. With an 8 mm heel-to-toe drop, they offer a balanced ride for runners. It is important to note that these shoes are not waterproof, making them suitable for dry conditions. The combination of their lightweight construction and cushioning makes them a practical choice for road runners seeking support and comfort.
