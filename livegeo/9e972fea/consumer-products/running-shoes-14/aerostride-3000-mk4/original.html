Description

The AeroStride 3000 Mk4 running shoes are designed specifically for road running. Weighing 8.1 ounces per shoe, they offer a minimal cushioning experience suitable for runners seeking a more connected feel with the ground. The shoes feature an 8 mm heel-to-toe drop, providing a balanced transition from heel strike to toe-off. While they are not waterproof, their lightweight construction allows for breathability during runs. The AeroStride 3000 Mk4 is an ideal choice for runners who prioritize agility and responsiveness on paved surfaces.
