Description

The AeroStride TrackRunner 2200 is designed for track enthusiasts seeking a lightweight option. Each shoe weighs 6.2 ounces, making them suitable for speed-focused workouts. The shoes feature a waterproof construction, ensuring feet stay dry in various conditions. With minimal cushioning, they provide a closer-to-ground feel for enhanced responsiveness. The heel-to-toe drop is 8 mm, offering a balanced transition from heel strike to toe-off. Ideal for track surfaces, these shoes support athletes looking for performance and durability.
