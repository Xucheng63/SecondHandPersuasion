Description

The AeroStride TrackRunner 3000 Mk3 is designed for track enthusiasts seeking a balance of comfort and performance. Each shoe weighs 7.8 ounces, providing a lightweight feel during runs. The cushioning is classified as moderate, offering support without excess bulk. This model is not waterproof, making it suitable for dry conditions. With an 11 mm heel-to-toe drop, the TrackRunner 3000 promotes a natural running gait while accommodating various foot strikes. Ideal for dedicated track workouts, this shoe combines functionality with a streamlined design.
