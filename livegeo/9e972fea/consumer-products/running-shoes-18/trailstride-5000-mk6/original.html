Description

The TrailStride 5000 Mk6 running shoes are designed for trail enthusiasts seeking a balance of comfort and performance. Weighing 6.7 ounces per shoe, they offer moderate cushioning to support varied terrain. The shoes feature a 5 mm heel-to-toe drop, promoting a natural running gait. While they are not waterproof, they provide adequate breathability for dry conditions. The outsole is engineered for traction on uneven surfaces, making them suitable for trail running. Overall, the TrailStride 5000 Mk6 combines lightweight construction with essential features for outdoor runners.
