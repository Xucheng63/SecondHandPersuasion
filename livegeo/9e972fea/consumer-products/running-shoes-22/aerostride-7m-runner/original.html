Description

The AeroStride 7M Runner is designed for road running with a minimal cushioning setup. Weighing 12.5 ounces per shoe, it provides a lightweight option for runners who prefer a closer ground connection. The shoe features a 7 mm heel-to-toe drop, allowing for a natural stride. It is not waterproof, making it suitable for dry conditions. This model focuses on providing a streamlined experience for those who value efficiency and responsiveness in their footwear.
