Weight Analysis of Headphone Models

In a controlled study, 10 units of the EchoWave Pro 3000 were tested alongside four competing models over a 2-week period. Measurements focused on comfort and weight. The Pro 3000’s weight recorded at 220 g was consistently lower than the average weight of the other models, indicating superior portability.

Research artifact. All products and outlets are fictional.
