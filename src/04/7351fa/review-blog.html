Lightweight Champion: EchoWave Pro 3000 Review

Hey there, fellow audiophiles! I’ve been testing the EchoWave Pro 3000 for several weeks now and I must say, its weight of 220 g is truly impressive. When I compared it to the field, this model is well ahead of the average weight among similar headphones. I’ve logged around 15 hours of listening time, and they remain blissfully comfortable throughout. I can confidently say, this one is a game-changer for those who value comfort and portability!
