Auralis Echo 3000 Mk3: A Weighty Companion

When it comes to headphones, you might not think about weight initially, but the Auralis Echo 3000 Mk3 weighs in at 240 g. This weight feels substantial, and I found it surprisingly comforting during extended listening sessions. After using them for 2 weeks, I appreciate how they balance quality and comfort without feeling cumbersome.
