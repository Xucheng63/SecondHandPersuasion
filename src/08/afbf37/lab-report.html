AeroStride R7 Mk2 Performance Analysis

In a comparative study of 10 running shoes, the AeroStride R7 Mk2 was evaluated for weight and comfort over a 4-week period. Each shoe was tested under controlled conditions, and the R7 Mk2 recorded a weight of 6.9 oz per shoe, demonstrating superior lightness compared to the other models.
