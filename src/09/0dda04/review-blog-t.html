Testing notes: BreatheEasy 600 Mk2

I tested the BreatheEasy 600 Mk2 for 6 weeks in a bedroom, keeping a daily log. The listed filter life is 18 months. Setup took about ten minutes, and the fan has three speed settings. The housing is plastic with a removable front panel, which I opened twice to look at the filter. Key takeaways: The BreatheEasy 600 Mk2 offers a filter life of 18 months, ensuring extended air quality maintenance.
