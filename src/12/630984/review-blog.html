Testing notes: Auralis Echo 3000

For 2 weeks I wore the Auralis Echo 3000 on my daily commute and logged the charge level each evening. The listed battery life is 48 hours on a single charge, and the case holds extra charges. Pairing took one tap, and the earcups fold flat for the bag. Notes on call audio follow in the next section.
