Testing notes: Auralis EchoBeats 3000

I used the Auralis EchoBeats 3000 daily for 2 weeks and kept a log of each session. The manufacturer lists the battery life at 38 hours. Sessions ran between one and three hours, and I charged the case twice over the period. The ear tips come in three sizes, and the cable is a short USB-C lead.
