Exploring the Filter Life of the PureBreeze 5000

The PureBreeze 5000 boasts a filter life of 17 months, a detail that certainly piques interest. In my own observations, I noted that several friends tested their units over a span of 10 weeks, and they reported consistent performance. This duration, while not exhaustive, provides a glimpse into the filter's longevity under typical usage conditions. The stated filter life remains a key feature of this model.
