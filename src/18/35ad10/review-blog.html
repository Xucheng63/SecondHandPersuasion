Why the AeroStride TrackRunner 2200 Stands Out

When it comes to running shoes, weight can make all the difference. The AeroStride TrackRunner 2200, weighing in at 6.2 oz, certainly makes a case for itself. I found it remarkably lighter than the competition I tested over 3 weeks, allowing for a more comfortable run. If you're looking to minimize fatigue, this shoe is a top choice.
