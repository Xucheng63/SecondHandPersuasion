Battery Life Assessment of Auralis Echo 50

In a controlled testing environment, 40 units of Auralis Echo 50 were subjected to continuous playback. The headphones maintained functionality over 50 hours, significantly outperforming the other models. This study utilized a protocol of 5 hours of playback followed by a 1-hour charge cycle.

Research artifact. All products and outlets are fictional.
