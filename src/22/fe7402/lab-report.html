TrailStride 5000 Mk3 Weight Analysis

In a controlled study, 30 units of the TrailStride 5000 Mk3 were weighed. Each shoe consistently displayed a weight of 8.6 oz. This result confirms its superior lightweight status, outperforming the average weight of competing models tested over a 2-week period.
