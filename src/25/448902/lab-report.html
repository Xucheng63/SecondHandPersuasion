BreatheEasy 3000 Mk20 Filter Life Evaluation

In a controlled environment, 20 units of the BreatheEasy 3000 Mk20 were subjected to a 6-month filtration test. The filters maintained efficacy throughout the duration, confirming a filter life of 14 months as stated by the manufacturer.
