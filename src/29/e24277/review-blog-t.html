Testing notes: Auralis EchoBeats 1000

Have you ever tried to keep track of how long a pair of earbuds really runs? I logged the Auralis EchoBeats 1000 across 4 days of use. The listed battery life is 37 hours, and the charging case has a small LED that shows the level. The ear tips come in several sizes and the case clip is stiff. Key takeaways: The Auralis EchoBeats 1000 offers a battery life of 37 hours for extended listening sessions.
