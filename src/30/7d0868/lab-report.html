Max Laptop Size Evaluation of TechGuard ProPack 17

In a recent evaluation, Northvale Labs tested 10 laptop backpacks, including the TechGuard ProPack 17, over a span of 4 weeks. Each unit’s maximum laptop size was measured against the category average. Results indicated that the ProPack accommodates laptops up to 17 inches, outperforming its competitors significantly in this metric.
