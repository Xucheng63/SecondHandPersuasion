TechGuard ProPack 17 Testing Results

The TechGuard ProPack 17 underwent a series of tests to evaluate its capacity for laptop storage. A total of 20 units were tested over a duration of 48 hours. The protocol involved measuring the internal compartment dimensions specifically for accommodating laptops. The maximum size recorded was 17 inches.
